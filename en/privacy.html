Privacy & terms

Your site notes.
Your device.

FieldMark does not collect data, upload photos, or include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. There is no account or app server. Photos, measurements, notes and projects are stored in this app’s private storage.

You choose individual images with Apple’s photo/file pickers or take a photo with your permission. Files leave the app when you choose to share or export. The receiving app’s policy applies. Your device’s backup settings may include app data.

Delete photos or projects to remove their local files. Uninstalling removes local app data. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately. Before changing devices or uninstalling, save an editable project backup somewhere safe.

Apple handles purchases; FieldMark verifies signed StoreKit entitlements and receives no payment details. Purchase and restore require App Store connectivity. Offline editing and exporting do not.

Support email messages and your reply address are used to handle your request. Contact the developer to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements. Avoid sending private project files unless you want support to inspect them.
