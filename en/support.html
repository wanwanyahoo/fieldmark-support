A quick field guide

1. Create a project and add a photo from Photos, Files or the camera. Assign a room in Photo details.

2. Choose Dimension. Draw a line or tap its two endpoints, then enter your tape or laser reading. Each new line opens the input straight away.

3. Pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to pan. In Select mode, drag endpoints or the label. Snap keeps dimensions horizontal or vertical. Lock finished annotations to prevent accidental moves.

4. Angle: tap the first arm, vertex, then second arm, and enter the angle you measured. Photo perspective can distort angles.

5. Mark uncertain readings Recheck and completed checks Verified. Add a detail photo and caption for anything hard to explain.

6. Share a PNG/JPEG from the editor or a multi-photo PDF from the project. PDF includes a text measurement register, notes and detail photos. Choose A4 or US Letter.

7. Export an editable .fieldmark backup regularly. A PDF or picture cannot restore an editable project. Restore from the home screen or open a .fieldmark file in Files.

Batch import photos, select and reorder photos for PDF, ZIP or CSV export. Attach up to eight detail photos and link them to annotations. Save sums, areas and volumes from entered readings.

Lifetime access / restore purchases

Lifetime removes the personal record creation limit for photos and PDF pages. All tools and exports are also free. The example does not use your free slots.

Free: 2 personal records (photos or PDF pages), all tools and exports. Restored backups remain editable and exportable without purchase. Existing work stays available.

*Per project: 50 records (photos or PDF pages), 75 MB of assets, 200 annotations per record and 20 layers. Subject to available device storage.

Apple handles purchases; FieldMark verifies signed StoreKit entitlements and receives no payment details. Purchase and restore require App Store connectivity. Offline editing and exporting do not.

Photo measurement

Set a known length or rectangle in Photo measurement to estimate other lengths on the same plane. Estimates show ≈ and update when endpoints move. Without a reference, enter actual readings manually. AR scans and laser connections are not included. Verify on site before fabrication or ordering.

Import PDF plans

Import PDF plans from More photo sources. Each selected page becomes a record. Set Drawing scale (1:N), or calibrate using a known length. Use Layers to show, hide, lock and reorder annotations. Annotated plan PDF keeps the page size; print at 100% and verify against a known dimension. Cropping is not redaction.
